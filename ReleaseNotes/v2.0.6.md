# OneLyrics Suite v2.0.6

### Bug Fixes
- **Gurmukhi & Punjabi Transliteration Fix:** Fixed missing Gurmukhi character mappings (including `ਲ਼` LLA, nukta pair-bindi consonants, composite vowel bearers) ensuring complete transliteration into Latin/English script without residual Punjabi glyphs.
- **Auto-Update & Re-sync:** Project store now automatically detects and re-transliterates any untransliterated Indic characters upon opening, and added a manual "Re-sync" button in the Asset Sidebar.
- **Word Ending Precision:** Fixed schwa deletion before punctuation marks and prevented truncation of common words like "Hun" and "Sun".
