# OneLyrics v1.2.0

## What's New
- **Integrated YouTube Publisher**: Direct upload, scheduling, and metadata management for YouTube natively within OneLyrics.
- **Background Uploads**: Seamlessly continue to use the app while your lyric videos upload or queue.
- **Recurring Publishing Schedules**: Set rules to automatically push your queued videos to YouTube on specific days and times.
- **Studio-Level Metadata Sync**: Automated Title and Description templates, tag generation, and visibility settings out of the box.

## Fixes & Polish
- Fixed an issue where the YouTube Queue would occasionally deadlock unscheduled videos into a Draft state.
- Fixed a bug where upload progress bars were not updating in real-time.
- Polished Export Modal workflow to instantly segue into YouTube Publishing without needing external browsers.
